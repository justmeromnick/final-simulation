12 V DC model · 10 Ω lamps — assumptions & practical guidance

Readings use a 12 V DC source with 0.10 Ω internal resistance and fixed 10 Ω lamps. Closed switches and wires have zero resistance in the main model. Loaded source voltage may be below 12 V.

Wire colours update automatically: red positive feed, black negative return, yellow incorrect, blue no current. Blue does not mean a physical conductor is safe to touch. Incorrect connections remain editable until the level is complete.

Real lamps change resistance as they heat. This simplified model does not predict heating, inrush, or component damage. Brightness approximates calculated power.

The green indicator means the labelled terminal networks match this lesson. Equivalent junction wiring is accepted; other terminal assignments may be rejected. All required switches must be ON to complete a level.

This is a virtual learning activity. Physical wiring requires separate instructor-approved procedures and suitable equipment. The game does not provide installation or safety certification.
